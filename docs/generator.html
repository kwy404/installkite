The cutting mat

PWA manifest and icon generator

Drop your logo, name your app and download a zip with manifest.webmanifest, every icon size, maskable icons, apple-touch-icon.png, favicon.ico and the head tags. It all happens in your browser: the image never leaves your device.

Maskable icon

The dashed circle is the safe zone: 80% of the icon. installkite measures your artwork and scales it so its farthest visible pixel stays inside.

Splash screen

Android builds the splash screen from background_color, the largest icon and name. The title bar and status bar take theme_color.

Prefer the command line?

The same icons, plus a service worker

The generator gives you the manifest and icons. npx installkite init also writes the service worker with an offline page, injects the head tags into every page and checks the result with doctor.
